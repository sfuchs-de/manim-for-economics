# Empirical-result-led storyboard template

Use this when the paper's contribution is primarily a measured effect,
descriptive fact, event study, decomposition, or heterogeneous response.

| Time | Learning goal | On-screen words | Visual action | Conceptual handoff | Evidence/status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:06 | Pose the empirical question | One outcome and one comparison | Show the estimand in words | The question defines what must vary | Motivation |
| 0:06–0:18 | Define the sample and unit | who · where · when | Build one concrete observation or sampling frame | The unit anchors all later charts | Data + sample |
| 0:18–0:32 | Explain identifying variation | treatment vs comparison, before vs after, or instrument | Reveal the comparison without coefficients | The comparison identifies the response | Design |
| 0:32–0:47 | Show the central estimate | One result sentence | Draw the native coefficient, path, or contrast | The estimate answers the narrow question | Main table or figure |
| 0:47–1:01 | Show heterogeneity or mechanism | One economically motivated split | Hold axes and scale fixed across groups | Differences refine the interpretation | Prespecified result |
| 1:01–1:14 | Interpret magnitude | relative to [baseline] | Re-express the estimate in a meaningful unit | Magnitude becomes substantive, not merely significant | Calculation + manifest |
| 1:14–1:22 | Close | Supported takeaway | Return to the original comparison | End at the paper's identification frontier | Synthesis |

Do not animate conventional confidence intervals as decorative motion. Preserve
scales across comparisons and state the estimand, unit, baseline, and sample.
