# Recipe brief

## Learning goal

Show where high- and low-traffic links lie in the U.S. highway network, then
identify a small number of verified corridors without removing the full
distribution from view.

## Evidence status

The bundled example uses public Census boundaries and the paper's public-safe
derived 352-link geometry and traffic shares. The manifest records the units,
sources, transformations, and hashes. Restricted raw network inputs are not
included.

## Exclude

Do not use the recipe when link endpoints cannot be matched to the displayed
geography or when values use incomparable policy units.
