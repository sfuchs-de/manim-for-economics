# Recipe brief

## Learning goal

Show how a local impulse creates direct, first-round, and later responses before
the rounds are collected by a resolvent.

## Evidence status

The network and response weights are illustrative.

## Exclude

Do not claim that a complete nonlinear model is literally a Leontief system.
The recursion represents a defined linearized propagation block; other state
responses may enter the full equilibrium Jacobian.
