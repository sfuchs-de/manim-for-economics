# Agent-choice-welfare paper brief

## Audience

Who should understand the result, and what can they safely be assumed to know?

## One-sentence contribution

What does the paper establish about choice, adjustment, heterogeneity, welfare,
or policy?

## Decision maker and choice menu

Who or what chooses? List only the alternatives that carry the argument. State
whether choices are static or dynamic and which constraints matter.

## Change or comparison

What shock, treatment, policy, state, or information change alters the menu or
payoffs? What is the baseline?

## Heterogeneity and evidence

Which economic state predicts different responses? What variation identifies or
disciplines the response margins? List sources for every displayed result.

## Welfare or policy object

For whom, relative to what, and over what horizon is welfare measured? Which
terms are first order, which capture curvature, risk, option value, or
interactions, and which are not identified?

## Visual vocabulary and exclusions

Assign stable colors to choice margins and reuse them in evidence and welfare.
List interpretations the design, model, or approximation does not support.
