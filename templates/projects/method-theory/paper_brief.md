# Paper brief

## Audience

Who should understand the method or theorem, and which definitions can be
assumed?

## Contribution

Write one sentence naming the problem, the operation, and what becomes
identifiable, computable, measurable, or interpretable.

## Persistent object

Define the mathematical or economic object that survives every beat. List only
the inputs and notation used in the video.

## Key condition and operation

State the assumptions and transformation in economic language before formal
notation. Explain why each condition is needed.

## Result

State the theorem, estimator, sufficient statistic, algorithm, or solution in
words. Record the exact proposition, equation, or appendix source.

## Intuition

Choose one comparative static, counterexample, or restriction that reveals why
the result works.

## Application

Explain what the result lets a researcher or policymaker do.

## Claims to exclude

List conclusions that fail without the stated conditions or lie outside the
result.
