# Paper brief

## Audience

Who should understand the empirical result, and what can they be assumed to
know about the design?

## Contribution

Write one sentence naming the outcome, comparison, and supported interpretation.

## Persistent estimand

Define the outcome, treatment or exposure, unit, sample, horizon, baseline, and
scale. Reuse the same words and semantic color throughout the video.

## Identifying variation

Explain in plain language what varies, what forms the comparison, and which
assumptions make the response interpretable.

## Central evidence

Name the result-producing table, figure, or replication output. Record the
confidence level and any transformation used for display.

## Heterogeneity or dynamics

Include only a split or path that refines the main interpretation. State whether
it was prespecified and keep scales comparable.

## Claims to exclude

List causal, external-validity, mechanism, or welfare claims that the design
does not establish.
