# Mechanism-led paper brief

## Audience

Who should understand the mechanism, and what can they safely be assumed to
know?

## One-sentence contribution

What does the paper establish about propagation, equilibrium, or system-wide
effects?

## Persistent system

What single network, market, allocation, model object, or institutional setting
will remain on screen? Which parts are empirical and which are schematic?

## Intervention

What changes? Is it a policy, shock, parameter, technology, information set, or
constraint? State its unit and baseline where relevant.

## Propagation channels

Name at most three channels in plain economic language. For each one, state
which persistent object changes and which result reveals its importance.

## Benchmark

What is the full or preferred case? Which restrictions, counterfactuals, or
alternative models are compared with it?

## Evidence and claims

List the source for every displayed value. State the interpretations the model,
design, or data do not support.

## Visual vocabulary

Assign stable colors to the intervention, each channel, and the outcome. State
which concrete and analytical views must update together.
