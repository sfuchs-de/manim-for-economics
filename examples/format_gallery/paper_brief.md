# Paper brief

This is a component gallery, not a paper explainer.

## Audience

Economists choosing a visual and narrative format before writing scene code.

## Contribution

Demonstrate three reusable formats: a causal chain, two synchronized views of
one economic state, and a benchmark-centered comparison.

## Evidence

No empirical evidence is presented. The comparison values are illustrative and
exist only to demonstrate geometry.

## Claims to exclude

- The example values are not estimates.
- The network is not calibrated to a transport or labor-market model.
- The visual formats do not determine the paper's narrative; they support an
  already approved economic argument.
