# Visual review

## Sample frames

Include:

- One settled frame per beat.
- The midpoint of every major transform.
- One frame immediately before and after each title or equation replacement.
- The first and last frames.

## Inspect

- Title and caption safe areas.
- Centering and symmetry of repeated objects.
- Arrow origins, endpoints, crossings, and labels.
- Stable axis ranges and readable direct labels.
- Source notes and qualifiers.
- Brief duplicate text caused by overlapping fade and transform objects.
- Partially revealed equations that read as unintended claims.
- Hold time after the final element appears.

## Fix order

1. Remove simultaneous content that is not needed.
2. Split a beat when two learning goals compete.
3. Reposition labels and paths.
4. Shorten prose.
5. Reduce type size only as a last resort.

Re-render the affected transition and one neighboring settled frame after each
structural fix.
