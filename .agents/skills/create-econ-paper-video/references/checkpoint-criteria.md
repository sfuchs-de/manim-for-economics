# Checkpoint criteria

## Brief approved

- One-sentence contribution is accurate and non-promotional.
- The persistent object, central change or comparison, result, and
  interpretation are named.
- Empirical results state estimand, sample, units, and identifying comparison.
- Welfare results, when present, state baseline, horizon, sample, units, and
  whether shock signs are combined or evaluated separately.
- Claims to exclude are explicit.
- The selected project template matches the contribution rather than merely
  reusing familiar subject matter.

## Storyboard approved

- Each beat has one learning goal.
- Every factual statement has a source.
- Illustrative material is labeled.
- The transition between adjacent beats advances the economic reasoning.
- Total timing includes readable holds, not only animation run times.
- The opening question and conclusion are logically compatible.

## Visual system approved

- A representative settled frame is legible at 854×480.
- Title, stage, caption, and source-note hierarchy is stable.
- Semantic colors do not change meaning.
- Recurring economic objects are recognizable across beats.
- Arrow geometry and direct labels remain clean during transitions.

## Preview approved

- All scenes render in sequence.
- Settled and transition contact sheets have been inspected.
- No clipping, collision, duplicate fade, blank frame, or premature removal
  remains.
- Equations reveal incrementally with their corresponding margin.
- Empirical values match the claim/source crosswalk.

## Delivery approved

- 1920×1080 at the declared frame rate.
- Video decodes and duration is nonzero.
- Final contact sheet passes.
- Silent master has no audio stream.
- Optional audio has documented rights and attribution.
